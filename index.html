About
Eli Colburn

  • Format: Open-Format, Vinyl Preferenced
  • Specialty: Private, Club, Corporate
  • Education: B.S. Audio Engineering (MTSU)

Eli Colburn is a DJ, music producer, audio engineer, and musician in Greenville, South Carolina. Eli started playing guitar as a preteen and began his DJ experience with the purchase of the Numark DJ Battle Pak with his high school graduation money. He graduated from Middle Tennessee State University with a degree in the Music Industry with a focus in Audio Engineering.

With a decade plus of DJ experience Eli has done sets with everything from just a laptop to only vinyl records, the latter being his preference for spinning recently. Vinyl has a specific quality on a proper sound system, which Eli brings to his gigs to make sure the experience is of the highest audiophile quality. Eli specializes in private events, clubs, and corporate events where he plays for the room and sets the vibe.
